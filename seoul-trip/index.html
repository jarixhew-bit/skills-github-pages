天气Weather首尔 1 月平均气温 −2.4°C，平均高温 1.5°C、低温 −5.9°C（韩国气象厅气候平年值）。要带羽绒外套、手套、毛帽和暖暖包。页面上每天的天气条在出发前约两周开始显示预报。January in Seoul averages −2.4°C, with an average high of 1.5°C and low of −5.9°C (Korea Meteorological Administration climate normals). Bring a down jacket, gloves, a woolly hat and heat packs. The daily weather line on each day starts showing a forecast about two weeks before departure.
货币Money韩元（KRW）。约 1 美元 ≈ 1,340 韩元（2026 年 11 月的汇率，出发前再看一次）。South Korean won (KRW). About USD 1 ≈ KRW 1,340 (the November 2026 rate; check again before you go).
