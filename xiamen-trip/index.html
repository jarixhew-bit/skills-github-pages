鼓浪屿船票也要先买Book the Gulangyu ferry in advance too

白天游客从厦门国际邮轮中心 厦鼓码头上船（不是老轮渡码头），35元／人，买去程送回程票（20天内有效一次）。购票走官方微信公众号或支付宝搜「厦门轮渡有限公司」，也可现场买但旺季会没票。提前30–60分钟到码头候船，返程从内厝澳码头或钢琴码头上船。By day, visitors sail from the Xiamen International Cruise Terminal (Xiagu Pier) — not the old downtown ferry pier — at ¥35 per person, with the return leg included (valid once within 20 days). Buy through the operator's official WeChat account or Alipay ("厦门轮渡有限公司"); counter sales exist but sell out in peak season. Be at the pier 30–60 minutes early; coming back, board at Neicuo'ao or Piano Pier.

手机支付：出发前先绑好卡Mobile payment: link a card before you fly

厦门几乎所有店（包括大排档和市场摊位）都收支付宝／微信，现金反而找零麻烦。支付宝和微信都能绑外国 Visa／Mastercard，但绑定要验证、有时要几分钟到几小时，在家先弄好，别落地才试。小额消费免手续费，大额会收 3%。Almost everything in Xiamen — street stalls and wet markets included — takes Alipay or WeChat Pay; cash is the awkward option. Both apps accept foreign Visa/Mastercard, but verification can take minutes to hours, so set it up at home, not on arrival. Small payments are fee-free; larger ones carry about 3%.
🚕

交通：打车 + 地铁Getting around: taxi and metro

用高德地图或滴滴叫车最省事（App 内直接付款，不用讲中文）。高崎机场 T3 → 思明市区约 10–15 公里，车程 20–30 分钟、约 30–45 元；T3 到达层 7 号门外排队上出租车。机场没有直通地铁，要先坐公交或 BRT 换乘。市区地铁末班约 22:30。The easiest option is hailing a car in Amap or Didi (pay in-app, no Chinese needed). Gaoqi T3 to the Siming city area is 10–15 km, 20–30 minutes, about ¥30–45; the taxi rank is outside door 7 on the arrivals level. There is no metro station at the airport — you would have to bus or BRT to one. Last metro in the city is around 22:30.
📶

上网：大马号码开漫游，或买中国 eSIMData: roam on your Malaysian number, or buy a China eSIM

中国境内连不上 Google、WhatsApp、Instagram。用大马／柬埔寨号码国际漫游时流量走境外出口，这些 App 照常能用，是最省事的做法；买中国本地 SIM 卡反而会被挡。中国 eSIM 也要选写明「国际路由」的那种。Google, WhatsApp and Instagram are blocked on mainland networks. International roaming on your Malaysian or Cambodian number routes data offshore, so those apps keep working — much the simplest route; a local Chinese SIM would be blocked. If you buy a China eSIM, pick one that states it routes internationally.
🌦

八月的厦门：热、湿、台风季Xiamen in August: hot, humid, typhoon season

白天 30–34°C，湿度高，午后常有雷阵雨。8–9月是台风季，台风来时轮渡会全线停航、鼓浪屿上不去——出发前一天看一次天气，行程里 D3 的鼓浪屿和 D2 的商场可以对调。防晒、伞、轻便透气衣物必备。Daytime 30–34°C, high humidity, frequent afternoon thunderstorms. August–September is typhoon season, and a typhoon suspends every ferry — Gulangyu simply closes. Check the forecast the day before; D3 (Gulangyu) and D2 (malls) can swap if needed. Bring sunscreen, an umbrella and light breathable clothes.
🛂

入境：马来西亚护照免签30天Entry: 30-day visa-free for Malaysian passports

中马互免签证，持马来西亚普通护照赴中国旅游可停留30天，这趟只待3晚，绰绰有余。落地后填的入境卡与健康申报现在多为扫码线上填，T3 有指示牌。⚠ 免签政策会调整，出发前一周再看一次中国驻马大使馆公告。Malaysia and China waive visas for each other, so an ordinary Malaysian passport gets 30 days for tourism — far more than these 3 nights. Arrival and health declarations are now mostly filled in online via QR code; signage at T3 points the way. ⚠ Visa-waiver terms do change — check the Chinese Embassy in Malaysia notice the week before you fly.

08:30–12:30 鼓浪屿08:30–12:30 Gulangyu Island

世界文化遗产，岛上全岛禁行机动车，只能走路——所以穿好走的鞋。经典路线：日光岩（全岛制高点，看厦门全景，另收门票）→ 菽庄花园＋钢琴博物馆（临海园林）→ 万国建筑群的老别墅巷子 → 龙头路吃小吃买手信。12:30 前往钢琴码头或内厝澳码头坐回程船。A UNESCO World Heritage site with no motor vehicles anywhere on the island — it is all on foot, so wear proper shoes. The classic loop: Sunlight Rock (the high point, panorama over Xiamen, separate ticket) → Shuzhuang Garden and the Piano Museum (seafront garden) → the lanes of old international-style villas → Longtou Road for snacks and souvenirs. Head to Piano Pier or Neicuo'ao Pier by 12:30 for the boat back.
