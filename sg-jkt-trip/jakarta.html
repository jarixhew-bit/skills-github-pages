红树林公园 Taman Wisata Alam Angke KapukAngke Kapuk Mangrove Park (Taman Wisata Alam Angke Kapuk)

⭐4.3（Google 16,505 条评价）。地址：Jl. Mualim Teko, Kamal Muara, Penjaringan, Jakarta Utara 14470。市区里少见的一大片红树林，木栈道和竹片步道穿过林子、绕着湖面，安静、绿意多，慢慢走一圈约一小时。建议上午去：中午后热，午后常下雨，下过雨步道会泥泞湿滑。
11/15（日）开放时间：07:00–18:00
门票：本地成人周末每人 Rp35,000，外国游客票价较高，以售票处公告为准

A rare stretch of mangrove forest inside the city, with wooden and bamboo boardwalks winding through the trees and around a lake — quiet and green; a slow loop takes about an hour. Go in the morning: it gets hot after midday, rain is common in the afternoon, and the paths turn muddy and slippery after rain.
Sun 15 Nov hours: 07:00–18:00
Admission: Rp35,000 per local adult at weekends; foreign visitors pay a higher rate — check the ticket counter

民族独立纪念碑 MonasNational Monument (Monas)

⭐4.6（Google 122,639 条评价）。地址：Jl. Lapangan Monas, Gambir, Jakarta Pusat 10110（独立广场 Merdeka Square）。雅加达的地标，132 米高的白色方尖碑，顶端是金色火焰。可以只在广场上看碑、拍照；想上去的话，碑座里有历史场景模型展厅，坐电梯可到顶上的观景台俯瞰市区（登顶每天限量，越早去越好排）。周一闭馆，周日开放。
11/15（日）开放时间：纪念碑 08:00–22:00；广场早上 06:00 起
门票：广场免费；碑座展厅 Rp8,000、登顶 Rp24,000（成人）。售票只收 JakCard 储值卡，现场柜台可买卡
离酒店：车程约 45–70 分钟⭐4.6 (122,639 Google reviews). Jl. Lapangan Monas, Gambir, Jakarta Pusat 10110 (Merdeka Square). Jakarta's landmark: a 132-metre white obelisk topped with a golden flame. You can simply see it and take photos from the square; if you want to go in, the base holds a hall of historical dioramas and a lift goes up to a viewing deck over the city (the summit has a daily cap, so earlier is better). Closed on Mondays, open on Sundays.
Sun 15 Nov hours: monument 08:00–22:00; the square from 06:00
Admission: the square is free; base exhibition Rp8,000, summit Rp24,000 (adults). Tickets are sold only on a JakCard stored-value card, which you can buy at the counter
From the hotel: about 45–70 minutes by car

⭐4.6（Google 29,983 条评价）。地址：Jl. Lada Dalam No.7, Pinangsia, Taman Sari, Jakarta Barat 11110。荷兰殖民时期留下的老城中心，大广场四周是白墙红瓦的老建筑，只逛广场和建筑、不进博物馆，半小时到一小时就够。周日人多、很热闹，广场上没有遮阴，带伞和水。

The heart of the Dutch colonial old town: a big square ringed by white-walled, red-roofed old buildings. Just walk the square and look at the buildings — no need to go into the museums; half an hour to an hour is enough. It is busy and lively on Sundays, and the square has no shade, so bring an umbrella and water.
