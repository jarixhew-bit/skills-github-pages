拉面主题馆，地下楼层完整重现 1958 年的昭和街景，气氛很好拍。馆内集合日本各地知名拉面名店的分店（会定期更换），每家都有迷你碗（约 700 日元起），可以一次吃好几家比较——入场费不含拉面钱，面另计。有素食拉面（约 1050 日元）与不含动物性食材的无叉烧版本（约 1100 日元），但官网写明未取得清真认证，忌口的话点餐时请再跟店员确认汤底。多数柜台可刷卡，少数（停车场、零食店、占卜）限现金。个人参观不必预约，当天现场买票即可。位于横滨新横滨，距银座约30公里、车程约45–60分钟（估算，未精确核实），当天要留出足够时间往返A ramen theme museum whose basement recreates a 1958 Showa-era streetscape — very photogenic. It gathers branches of famous ramen shops from across Japan (rotated periodically), and each serves mini bowls (from about ¥700) so you can try several in one visit — ramen is charged separately from admission. Vegetarian ramen (about ¥1,050) and an animal-free, chashu-less version (about ¥1,100) are available, though the official site states it is not Halal certified, so confirm broth ingredients with staff if anyone has restrictions. Most counters take cards; a few (parking, the sweets shop, fortune telling) are cash only. No reservation needed for individuals — buy tickets on arrival. Located in Shin-Yokohama — about 30 km from Ginza, roughly 45–60 min by car (estimated, not precisely verified), so allow enough time for the round trip.
